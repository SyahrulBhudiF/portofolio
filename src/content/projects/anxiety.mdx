The system extracts X, Y, theta, and magnitude micro-movement features from 7×7 POC-ABS blocks across the right eye-eyebrow, left eye-eyebrow, and mouth regions. A TabR classifier separates low and high anxiety levels, reaching 58% accuracy in evaluation. Results are validated against questionnaires and expert-defined anxiety groupings.

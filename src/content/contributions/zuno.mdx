highlights:
  - Removed the Linux decorations override and defaulted native window controls off so the app's own title bar is used.
  - Hid the app-drawn window buttons on tiling compositors (niri, sway, hyprland) where the WM owns window management, while GNOME/KDE keep close and minimize reachable.
  - Added a Linux-only media-session toggle and gated the browser mediaSession bridge to Linux, since WebView2 and WKWebView already bridge it to SMTC and MPNowPlayingInfoCenter on their own.
  - Cleared navigator.mediaSession metadata, playback state, and action handlers whenever the bridge is disabled so no duplicate now-playing entry survives.
